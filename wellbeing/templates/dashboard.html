How to read this dashboard

Where the numbers come from

Collection
The Usage Dump app on the phone records Android's own usage events every 6 hours and uploads them to Google Drive. Opening this page, or Get phone data, reads the latest upload, so the newest data can be up to about 6 hours old. Run now in the app makes it current.
Days and hours
Cut in the phone's time zone (on the PC, the zone the archive was set up in). A day runs from midnight to midnight.
Date range
Today and Yesterday, or any range with the two date boxes. Click a bar in "Screen time per day" to see that day alone.

Screen time and apps

Screen time
Time an app was on screen in the foreground. It stops when the screen turns off or the phone locks, so an app left open overnight doesn't count.
System UI
The status bar, keyboard, permission pop-ups and edge panel are left out unless count system UI is ticked. The home screen (One UI Home) is counted.
Opened / visit
One stretch of an app on screen. Leaving and coming back within 5 seconds (e.g. switching screens inside the app) is the same visit.
Typical visit
The median visit: half the visits were shorter, half longer.
Longest sitting
The single longest visit. A visit is capped at 6 hours, because anything longer means the phone never recorded its end.
What it connects to
The app used immediately before and after, skipping the home screen and system UI in between.
Excluded apps
Apps unticked under "Exclude apps from totals" vanish from every app-based figure. Unlocks, notifications and pick-up counts are about the phone, so they don't change.

Unlocks and notifications

Unlocks
Each time the lock screen was dismissed (PIN, fingerprint or face).
Notifications
Notifications that interrupt: shown with sound, vibration or a pop-up. Silent ones aren't counted.

Assumptions about your day

Waking day
16 hours. "Share of a 16h waking day" is screen time divided by 16 hours, since most of the other 8 are sleep.
Wake-up time
The first time any app was used that day.
Bedtime
The last time any app was used that day.
First thing on waking
An app counts if its first use came within 10 minutes of the phone's first use that day.
Last thing before sleep
An app counts if its last use came within 10 minutes of the phone's last use that day.
Parts of the day
Night 23:30–07:00 · Morning 07:00–12:00 · Afternoon 12:00–17:00 · Evening 17:00–20:00 · Late 20:00–22:30 · Pre-bed 22:30–23:30.

The day boundary is midnight, not sleep. If you use the phone at 00:30 before sleeping, that counts as the next day's wake-up time, and the previous day's bedtime is the last use before midnight. Late nights therefore look like early starts.

Pick-ups

Pick-up
The screen came on and the phone was unlocked. It lasts from screen-on to screen-off.
Glance
The screen came on but the phone wasn't unlocked: checking the time or a notification on the lock screen.
Micro
A pick-up shorter than 30 seconds.
Impulse
A pick-up with no notification in the 2 minutes before the unlock. Only notifications since the phone was last locked count, so one you'd already seen doesn't explain a later pick-up.
Typical pick-up
The median length of a pick-up.
Where pick-ups lead
The first app opened after unlocking, skipping the home screen, keyboard and system UI.

The summary row's "unlocks under 30s" and "with no notification" are older, slightly different measures: they time from the unlock to the next lock, where the Pick-ups card times from screen-on to screen-off. A pick-up still in progress isn't counted until it ends, so today's pick-ups can trail today's unlocks.

Limits

History
The phone keeps detailed events for only about 10 days, so a day that was never collected in time is gone. Since the app collects every 6 hours, that only happens if the phone is off or the app is stopped for days.
Profiles
Only the main phone profile is read; Secure Folder and work profiles are not.
